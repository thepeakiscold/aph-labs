SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Engineering Highlights & Subsystems

01

2×2 Multi-Fan Active Flow Array: 4 independently controlled/synchronized high-response flow units.

02

Up to 40 m/s (144 km/h): Continuously variable velocity control from low-speed laminar onset to peak airspeed.

03

Rotational Wake Cancellation: Integrated downstream 3D aerodynamic stators eliminate swirl effects.

Multi-Fan Drive & Flow Conditioning

Four synchronized brushless electric flow units with digital ESCs drive continuously variable airspeeds up to 40 m/s (144 km/h), conditioned by downstream 3D anti-swirl stators and protective inlet/outlet FOD screens inside a Sigma aluminum T-slot frame.

Modulation Profiles & Research Integration

Supports steady-state hold, automated ramping, dynamic gusts, and stepped wind profiles over UART, PWM, CAN, and USB (Python & ROS 2 API ready) for propeller testing, pitot/airspeed calibration, and UAV HIL simulation.
